# Track 4 Publication Decision Record

Status: keep Track 4, but do not treat it as a core validated thesis claim yet.

## What Track 4 Is Good For Now

Track 4 is currently strongest as a visual and diagnostic traversal assay. It is
useful for showing whether the fused manifold supports cyclic movement, whether
anchors produce closed-loop returns, and whether observer-conditioned views
change path geometry. The refreshed focused leaf shows:

- 30 article trajectories available.
- 15 cyclic polarity paths across 3 anchors in the manifest.
- 6/15 polarity loops rendered under the visual cap.
- 100% closed-loop survival in the refreshed focused visual leaf.
- Observer Article 24 produces a visibly distinct centered manifold with
  axis rotation around 104.9 degrees and 28 path flips.

This is valuable evidence for inspection and hypothesis generation.

## What Track 4 Cannot Safely Claim Yet

Track 4 should not yet be framed as proving a general barrier/bridge traversal
law. The current focused thesis evidence still marks Track 4 traversal validity
and Track 4 work-barrier signal as unsafe. That does not mean Track 4 is useless;
it means the present evidence has not yet shown, across controls/seeds/kernels,
that work and closed-loop survival reliably distinguish semantic barriers from
measurement artifacts.

Avoid claims like:

- "Track 4 proves the manifold has real thermodynamic barriers."
- "Walker work is a validated semantic resistance metric."
- "Closed-loop failure identifies a topological void."
- "Track 4 alone validates the Riemannian geometry."

Use safer language:

- "Track 4 probes traversal behavior over the fused manifold."
- "Track 4 provides path-ensemble diagnostics used for visual and exploratory
  stress testing."
- "Track 4 currently supports artifact inspection; barrier claims require
  Markov/TPT validation against controls."

## Keep/Improve/Promote Criteria

Keep Track 4 in the system when it satisfies:

- Real cyclic paths are persisted and rendered without fabrication.
- `work_integral` and `closed_loop` are exported per path.
- Anchor selection is explicit and terrain-aware.
- Path-touched zone summaries exist.
- Observer-centered renders expose non-placeholder path changes.

Promote Track 4 toward a publishable claim only when it satisfies:

- Real/control differences in work and survival hold across `rbf`, `matern`,
  and `imq`.
- Shuffled/random controls show different path-touched zone flux from real data.
- Committor, MFPT, and reactive-flux summaries are finite and non-placeholder.
- Dominant reactive paths are stable across seeds.
- Track 4 metrics improve or explain Track 5/observer findings rather than
  merely decorating them.

Demote Track 4 to visualization-only if:

- Work/survival remains indistinguishable from controls.
- Path behavior depends primarily on rendering caps, anchor choice, or kernel
  artifacts.
- Observer path flips are zero/placeholder in canonical focused evidence.

## Publication Roadmap

  evidence.
- Keep Track 4 visible in MONOLITH but label it as traversal diagnostics.
- Include `work_integral`, `closed_loop`, hot/cold class, and anchor terrain in
  path hover/audit panels.

Mid term:

- Compare Track 4 real vs shuffled/random controls across the focused kernels.
- Export dominant reactive paths and path-touched zone flux.

Long term:

- Treat manifold Langevin, mMALA, or RMHMC proposal dynamics as an ablation
  branch, not the default thesis path.
- Only replace the discrete walker if the Markov/TPT layer first proves that the
  traversal diagnostics are scientifically useful.

## Current Paper Position

Track 4 should be presented as an exploratory traversal layer that generates
stress-test telemetry and visual audit trails. The publishable core should remain
the stronger evidence: synthetic recovery, observer relativity, Procrustes/control
separation, and Track 5 ablation coverage. Track 4 can become a future paper
contribution if the Markov/TPT diagnostics validate the traversal interpretation.
